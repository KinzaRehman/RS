Lobster How-To

How to eat the whole thing.

First time ordering the whole dinner? Here's the order of operations. Take your time- the tide isn't going anywhere.

  1. 1

    Claws First

    Twist them off and crack them open. The knucke meat next door is the sweetest bite in the whole lobster.

  2. 2

    Take the tail.

    Twist it away from the body and snap off the little flippers at the end.

  3. 3

    Push, don't dig.

    Push the tail meant out in one piece with your fork. Thumbs also work. Nobodys Judging.

  4. 4

    Dont skip the legs.

    Bite down gently and squeeze the mean out like a straw. This is where the locals live.
